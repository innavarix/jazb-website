New in v1.2.0

The Governance Core

Thirteen governance outcomes (G01 to G13) set out what an organization’s governing body must decide, oversee and answer for: executive accountability, risk appetite, delegated Authority, third-party dependencies, incident and crisis decision rights, regulatory obligations and more.

Governance assurance cannot come from technical controls alone. Applicable outcomes must be independently assessed and Verified before a conformance claim can be made.

Assessment

Built to be assessed

Every requirement has specific acceptance criteria, rationale and individually assessable components. The Assurance Integrity Model, three assessment baselines, an evidence-anchored maturity model and an OSCAL representation support consistent, machine-readable assessment.

Assurance discipline

Evidence over assertion

Assessment outcomes require appropriate evidence. Publication and internal qualification do not by themselves establish independent validation or operational effectiveness.

The AI Extension adds AI-specific cybersecurity requirements to JAZB-CYB. It complements the Core rather than replacing it.

Which framework do you need? Use the AI Extension to secure AI systems within your cybersecurity program. Use AI Governance & Enforcement to govern what AI is authorized to do.

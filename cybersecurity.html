Cybersecurity
Governance & Assurance.

JAZB Cybersecurity Framework v1.1.1 establishes consequence-based governance and evidence-bounded assurance. It complements established cybersecurity standards and remains internally qualified research, with independent and operational validation outstanding.

Assurance Integrity Model

Defensible security claims

Admissible evidence, behavioral verification, defined outcome states, deterministic roll-up, risk-acceptance non-conversion, freshness requirements, and common-cause dependency scrutiny.

Consequence-based obligations

Three cumulative baselines

Foundational: 62 requirements. Managed: 150 cumulative. High-Consequence: 172 cumulative. Four consequence tiers govern applicability rather than industry or organization size.

Assessment engineering

Practical, traceable assessment

Check-specific determination and failure criteria, sampling guidance, evidence-anchored capability levels, a structured Assessment Workbook, and machine-readable JSON and OSCAL resources.

Research limitations

Independent validation outstanding

The release has undergone internal consistency and architectural testing. It has not demonstrated enterprise operational effectiveness, independent assessor reliability, external human peer validation, or certification.

Publication status: Final public research edition. It is not independently or operationally validated. View version-specific DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24039940.

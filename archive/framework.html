JAZB v3.0.0 is a comprehensive enterprise AI governance and enforcement framework covering risk, lifecycle, trustworthiness, data protection, human impacts, legitimate Authority, and independent technical enforcement. Credentials, access, and capability alone do not establish legitimate Authority.

HUMAN-ESTABLISHED LAW

Law before machine action.

The Enterprise Domain Authority, or EDA, is JAZB's highest active human Authority for the Organization. Human-Established Law becomes governing only through a predefined lifecycle and a multi-human authorization threshold. AI may interpret established Law, operate under it, and recommend changes. AI may not establish Law or expand its own Authority.

Draft→Submit→Validate→Authorize→Establish→Activate

Human sovereignty

The Organization retains legitimate human sovereignty over the source and boundaries of JAZB Authority.

No AI legislation

An AI component cannot establish, authorize, activate, replace, revoke, or modify JAZB Law on its own Authority.

No self-expansion

Machine Authority remains bounded by purpose and Scope. An AI component cannot enlarge its own legitimate Authority.

Separation and evidence

Interpretation is separated from unrestricted execution, and material actions preserve sufficient provenance for review.

ONE FRAMEWORK. TWO POSSIBILITIES.

Comprehensive enterprise governance.
Bounded AI operation.

The published five-part JAZB v3.0.0 specification governs both organizational AI management and institutional enforcement. Both configurations preserve human sovereignty, independent inspection, judicial decisions, protective Actuation, Enterprise AI Customs, and evidence-based assurance.

Part I: Enterprise AI Management

Organizational objectives, AI inventory, risk management, governance policy, and human oversight.

Part II: AI Systems & Trustworthiness

AI lifecycle, evaluation, data protection, privacy, third parties, and affected persons.

Part III: Institutional Authority

Human-Established Law, Inspector, Judge, independent Actuation, Customs, and resilience.

Part IV: Core Operation & Assurance

Human-executed consequential business actions, technical integration, adoption, verification, and conformance.

Part V: Optional XAA Operation

Explicitly enrolled AI-initiated consequential transactions under exact Judicial Instruments and independent effect-time enforcement.

Core Operation

AI assists with analysis and proposals while humans perform consequential business actions. Independent protective enforcement is part of Core conformance.

XAA-Enabled Operation

The optional Executive Assistant AGENT coordinates only specifically authorized transactions under current Law, judicial authorization, and independent Actuation.

Evidence boundary: v3.0.0 defines 195 normative requirements and 77 verification scenarios. Publication does not establish operational validation or independent certification.

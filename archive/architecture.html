SEPARATION BY DESIGN

No component completes
the governance chain alone.

Under v3.0.0, organizational governance establishes AI purposes, risk boundaries, ownership, lifecycle controls, and human Law. The independent Inspector produces evidence, the Judge evaluates and issues dispositions, and independent Actuation validates and enforces permitted effects. Enterprise AI Customs retains independent Border passage authority. Core Operation keeps consequential business execution with humans; optional XAA Operation adds strictly instrumented, effect-time-checked AI-initiated transactions.

Enterprise Decision Rights

JAZB distinguishes business ownership, Law establishment, architecture, residual-risk acceptance, technical operation, expansion, and independent assurance.

Authority Lifecycle

Purpose and permissible autonomy precede Authority. Law, authorization, delegation, operation, reevaluation, revocation, renewal, and retirement remain governed.

Independent Jurisdictions

Internal Jurisdiction governs enterprise action. Border Jurisdiction governs lawful passage through EDA-registered internal data-domain Ports and the actual external Organizational Boundary. Authority in one does not silently authorize the other.

Independent Actuation

Interpretive decisions do not become unrestricted technical power. Actuation independently validates applicable active Law, the authorizing chain, effective target, and permitted effect before execution. A valid signature alone does not prove lawful action or technical prevention.

Continuous Assurance

Governance is continuously demonstrated through operation, observation, validation, discovery, remediation, and revalidation as systems and conditions change.

Chain of Authority & Evidence

Law, delegation, decisions, passage, actuation, exceptions, and recovery remain traceable to legitimate provenance and reconstructable evidence.

Lifecycle and Change

Establishment, operation, material change, reassessment, recovery, reauthorization, renewal, and retirement.

Current v3.0.0 behavioral boundary: The Inspector AGENT is evidentiary rather than self-authorizing. Its narrow single-device emergency isolation exception requires established standing Law and independent effect controls. XAA business actions require a current, exact Judicial Instrument and independent effect-time Actuation. Historical Task and Enforcer definitions remain preserved in the predecessor publications, not inherited as current v3.0.0 requirements.
Current State, Target State, and transition: Planned capabilities remain distinguishable from implemented and validated governance. Architecture decisions preserve accountable ownership, assumptions, dependencies, limitations, alternatives, evidence gates, rollback or exit conditions, and reassessment triggers.
Continuous Governance Assurance: Establish → Operate → Observe → Validate → Discover → Remediate → Revalidate. A successful prior test is not permanent assurance, and technical recovery does not automatically restore Authority.

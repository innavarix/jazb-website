FOUNDATIONAL GOVERNANCE

Assist without self-authorized execution.

Foundational AI assistants can retrieve authorized information, analyze records, prepare reports and drafts, and support human decision-making. The human remains responsible for consequential business execution. AI access is technically restricted to its actual delegated scope and tools, not merely instructed to remain within it.

Commissioner Law

Governs the assistant's authorized purpose, operational scope, task boundaries, and permitted assistance.

Commandant Law

Proposed as a foundational Law domain for governed end-user behavior, Point Policy thresholds, and authorized protective responses, without introducing an Enforcer AGENT.

Judge and Actuation

Preserve lawful adjudication, bounded interventions, and independent technical enforcement where required.

Customs and Evidence

Preserve governed passage, jurisdictional boundaries, attributable evidence, and continuous assurance.

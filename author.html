Michael Costner

Cybersecurity & AI Governance Researcher · CCSP, SSCP

See the frameworksAbout JAZB

Michael Costner is a cybersecurity systems engineer, framework developer, and technology entrepreneur with more than 30 years of experience in information technology, including nearly two decades supporting technology and security operations in regulated healthcare environments.

His work focuses on developing practical governance and assurance frameworks that address cybersecurity risk, artificial intelligence, organizational accountability, and the boundaries of delegated Authority.

As the creator of the JAZB AI Governance & Enforcement Framework and the JAZB Cybersecurity Framework, Michael applies a structured development approach encompassing architectural design, requirements engineering, adversarial scenario testing, assessment methodology, qualification, version control, and continuous refinement.

Building on that work, he is developing and working to implement the Framework Development Life Cycle (FDLC): a framework-agnostic methodology for engineering governance, risk, compliance, cybersecurity, and AI governance frameworks with the same discipline applied to mature software. FDLC defines lifecycle stages, deliverables, requirement traceability, assessment design, adversarial qualification, release governance, and maintenance for frameworks themselves. Its central principle is that publication is not validation: a framework should pass separate gates for publication readiness and for independent validation.

His research emphasizes the distinction between technical capability and legitimate Authority, with particular attention to human oversight, evidence-based assurance, and the governance challenges emerging from increasingly autonomous technologies.

Through independent research, framework development, and entrepreneurial initiatives, Michael seeks to advance cybersecurity and AI governance from conceptual principles into practical, assessable, and ultimately deployable systems.

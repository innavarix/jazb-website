The JAZB Virtual Tabletop Exercise series uses matched fictional environments to isolate JAZB-specific governance properties from controls the mature baseline already provides. The exercises are incident-informed structured tabletop analyses, not laboratory benchmarks or claims of universal prevention.

VIRTUAL TABLETOP EXERCISES

Three tests. Three different failure boundaries.

VTT-001

Unauthorized Production Action

Tests whether technical production capability can become legitimate destructive production Authority after a valid privileged credential is exposed.

PUBLISHED DISPOSITION Baseline: materially compromised. Full JAZB: destructive objective prevented.

VTT-002

Containment Escape and Unauthorized External Communication

Tests whether internal containment compromise and internet reachability can become lawful Border passage through Enterprise AI Customs.

PUBLISHED DISPOSITION Baseline may be materially compromised. Full JAZB prevents the external passage objective at the governed border.

VTT-003

Architecture Breaking Point Adversarial Stress Test

Escalates compromise across operational AI, credentials, interpretation, evidence, Border enforcement, actuation, Law workflows, emergency governance, and finally the human sovereign root.

PUBLISHED DISPOSITION No machine-originated architectural break identified within the tested conditions. The first valid Law-changing condition required capture of the legitimate EDA quorum.
Download VTT-003 ↓

Interpretation boundary: These exercises do not claim that JAZB would have prevented any named real-world incident. They test defined failure patterns under stated fictional assumptions and report only the differences attributable to JAZB within those conditions.

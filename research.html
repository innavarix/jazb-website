VALIDATION RESEARCH

Test the claims.
Find the breaking point.

The JAZB Validation and Threat Testing (VTT) program uses structured, incident-informed analysis to test Authority boundaries, identify failure conditions, document residual risk, and drive framework refinement. Testing includes Virtual Tabletop Exercises, matched fictional environments, adversarial scenarios, and retesting as the framework evolves. The results are bounded findings, not laboratory benchmarks or claims of universal prevention.

Unauthorized Production Action

VTT-004-v1.0

Objective Persistence and Unauthorized Access Circumvention

Tests whether a legitimately authorized AI research objective can become legitimate Authority to circumvent an external denial, obtain non-public information, or modify an external system when a technically viable alternate path exists.

PUBLISHED DISPOSITION PREVENTED under the stated full-implementation conditions. The authorized research objective did not become legitimate Authority for unauthorized means.

Interpretation boundary: These exercises may be informed by documented real-world incidents, but they test defined failure patterns within controlled test environments. Published dispositions apply only to the stated assumptions, controls, Authority boundaries, and implementation conditions of each exercise. They do not establish that JAZB was present in, or empirically tested against, the originating real-world system, and they do not constitute claims of universal prevention.

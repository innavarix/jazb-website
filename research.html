Research initiative · Conceptual development

Framework Development Life Cycle (FDLC)

A proposed, framework-agnostic engineering and assurance methodology for developing, qualifying, publishing, validating, and maintaining governance, risk, compliance, cybersecurity, and AI governance frameworks.

Research objective

Repeatable framework engineering

FDLC aims to define lifecycle stages, deliverables, requirement traceability, assessment design, adversarial qualification, release governance, and maintenance controls for frameworks themselves.

Core distinction

Publication is not validation.

A framework can be internally qualified for research publication without having demonstrated operational effectiveness or independent assessor reliability. FDLC proposes distinct gates for publication readiness and independent validation.

Status: Conceptual development. The JAZB research portfolio informs the proposed methodology, but JAZB-CYB was not originally developed under a formally established FDLC. The FDLC methodology has not yet been independently evaluated, adopted, or published as an industry standard. Originator: Michael Costner.

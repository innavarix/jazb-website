FROM JASB TO JAZB

JASB was the beginning.
JAZB is what emerged.

JASB began with a narrow governance problem: how to grant temporary elevated Authority for a legitimate task without converting temporary need into persistent privilege. As that work matured, the question moved beyond privileged access to the institutional source of Authority itself.

JASBPurpose-bound elevation→THE DEEPER QUESTIONWho established the governing rules and by what human Authority?→JAZBAuthority, Law, jurisdiction, passage, enforcement, and accountability
Authority-CentricHuman-SovereignTechnology-NeutralComprehensive Governance

PURPOSE AND STEWARDSHIP

A primary AI governance framework.
Independent enforcement by design.

JAZB v3.0.0 provides a comprehensive enterprise AI governance and enforcement specification spanning organizational risk, AI lifecycle, trustworthiness, human oversight, institutional Authority, and independent technical enforcement. It is designed for adoption as a primary AI governance framework while remaining interoperable with other standards, laws, and implementation technologies.

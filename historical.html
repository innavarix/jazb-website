Cybersecurity Framework v1.1.1 · November 7, 2026

Expanded from 21 to 28 domains (D22 to D28), 126 to 172 requirements and 338 to 420 acceptance checks, with 237 assessable parts. Introduced the Assurance Integrity Model, consequence tiers and materiality rules, three assessment baselines, an evidence-anchored maturity model, an OSCAL representation, and crosswalks to five standards. v1.0.0 identifiers were preserved. Superseded by Core v1.2.0.

Cybersecurity Framework v1.0.0 · November 5, 2026

The original JAZB-CYB publication established 21 cybersecurity domains, 126 normative requirements, and 338 acceptance checks. Internally qualified research, with independent and operational validation outstanding. Superseded by later Core editions, but preserved for citation and version-specific comparison.

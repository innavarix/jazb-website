EXPANDED ENFORCEMENT ARCHITECTURE

Consequential work must remain governed.

Expanded autonomy introduces Task AGENT execution under Commissioner Law, independently supervised conduct under Commandant Law, and separately controlled enforcement mechanisms. Neither role can establish its own Law or enlarge legitimate Authority.

Task AGENT

Performs explicitly delegated operations within approved purpose, scope, identity, timing, and permitted methods.

Enforcer AGENT

Independently evaluates relevant human and Task behavior, applying protected Point Policy evidence and permitted protective escalation.

Judge and Independent Actuation

Maintain established Authority boundaries, validate recipient and effective target, mediate consequential execution, and distinguish command issuance from verified effect.

Integrity, Resilience, and Assurance

Address active Law validation, independence, degraded operations, adversarial testing, recovery, and bounded evidence-based conformance claims.

Development status: v2.1 remains the internal working identifier. The intended public release identity is Enforcement Framework v1.0, subject to completion of normative reconciliation, source migration, and release governance. The historical JAZB Framework v2.0 record is not being relabeled.

NIST AI RMF 1.0 · CROSSWALK v1.0

JAZB Framework v1.1 to NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0

Provides an element-level mapping of all 72 NIST AI RMF 1.0 Core subcategories across GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE to the stable normative requirements of the JAZB Framework v1.1 Reference Specification.

VALIDATED MAPPING 72 of 72 AI RMF Core subcategories evaluated: 4 Direct Alignment, 29 Complementary Alignment, 4 Implementation Support, and 35 No Equivalence.

Formal author/internal validation was completed before publication. The crosswalk deliberately records No Equivalence where JAZB does not normatively address a broader AI risk-management outcome rather than extending JAZB claims beyond the framework's defined scope.

Version-specific scope: The published crosswalk evaluates JAZB Framework v1.1 against NIST AI RMF 1.0. Its classifications apply to those evaluated versions only. They do not automatically establish alignment or conformance for later JAZB Framework releases. Any updated mapping would require its own documented review and publication.

Interpretation boundary: The published crosswalk is an independent technical mapping intended to support analysis, implementation planning, research, and framework comparison. It does not establish certification, compliance, equivalence, approval, or endorsement by the organizations responsible for the external frameworks being mapped. Each external framework remains authoritative for its own requirements, terminology, scope, and conformance processes.

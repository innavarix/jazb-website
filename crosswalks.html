NIST AI RMF 1.0 to JAZB Framework v1.1

Assesses all 72 NIST AI RMF 1.0 Core subcategories against JAZB Framework v1.1. Classifications are conservative and include explicit No Equivalence findings. The mapping does not imply NIST approval or certification, and it does not cover v3.0.0. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23048446.

JAZB-CYB v1.2.0 to enterprise standards

The 172 requirements map to every NIST CSF 2.0 subcategory (106 of 106) and CIS Controls v8.1 safeguard (153 of 153), 91 of 93 ISO/IEC 27002:2022 controls, 35 of 40 COBIT 2019 objectives, and 194 NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 controls and enhancements. The 13 governance outcomes map to NIST CSF 2.0 Govern, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, COBIT 2019 and governing-body duties in law.

Relationships show overlapping subject matter to support traceability and evidence reuse, not equivalence, compliance, certification or endorsement. The v1.1.1 mappings remain in the v1.1.1 record.

THE FRAMEWORK

Capability does not
constitute Authority.

JAZB separates technical capability from legitimate Authority. Credentials, API access, administrative permissions, network reachability, or tool access may make an action possible. They do not make the action legitimate.

HUMAN-ESTABLISHED LAW

Law before machine action.

The Enterprise Domain Authority, or EDA, is JAZB's highest active human Authority for the Organization. Human-Established Law becomes governing only through a predefined lifecycle and a multi-human authorization threshold. AI may interpret established Law, operate under it, and recommend changes. AI may not establish Law or expand its own Authority.

Draft→Submit→Validate→Authorize→Establish→Activate

Human sovereignty

The Organization retains legitimate human sovereignty over the source and boundaries of JAZB Authority.

No AI legislation

An AI component cannot establish, authorize, activate, replace, revoke, or modify JAZB Law on its own Authority.

No self-expansion

Machine Authority remains bounded by purpose and Scope. An AI component cannot enlarge its own legitimate Authority.

Separation and evidence

Interpretation is separated from unrestricted execution, and material actions preserve sufficient provenance for review.

VERSION 1.3 | FRAMEWORK AND ARCHITECTURE CLARITY

Purpose becomes architecture.
Architecture governs action.

Authority must have an organizational basis before it has a technical realization. Version 1.3 distinguishes four related levels so that organizational governance remains traceable through architecture, operational functions, and enterprise technology.

JAZB Framework

Defines organizational purpose, doctrine, stakeholders, desired outcomes, governance, lifecycle, and assurance.

Enterprise Authority Architecture

Relates those concerns into a coherent model for legitimate, bounded machine Authority.

Authority Governance Functions

Preserve Law, interpretation, jurisdiction, bounded operation, Actuation, evidence, and assurance during operation.

Technical Realization

Uses products, services, controls, and platforms to implement the functions without becoming the source of Law or Authority.

A platform may implement several functions. Its capabilities alone do not establish JAZB conformance. Required separation, decision rights, Authority boundaries, and evidence independence must remain effective.

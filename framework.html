THE FRAMEWORK

Capability does not
constitute Authority.

JAZB separates technical capability from legitimate Authority. Credentials, API access, administrative permissions, network reachability, or tool access may make an action possible. They do not make the action legitimate.

HUMAN-ESTABLISHED LAW

Law before machine action.

The Enterprise Domain Authority, or EDA, is JAZB's highest active human Authority for the Organization. Human-Established Law becomes governing only through a predefined lifecycle and a multi-human authorization threshold. AI may interpret established Law, operate under it, and recommend changes. AI may not establish Law or expand its own Authority.

Draft→Submit→Validate→Authorize→Establish→Activate

Human sovereignty

The Organization retains legitimate human sovereignty over the source and boundaries of JAZB Authority.

No AI legislation

An AI component cannot establish, authorize, activate, replace, revoke, or modify JAZB Law on its own Authority.

No self-expansion

Machine Authority remains bounded by purpose and Scope. An AI component cannot enlarge its own legitimate Authority.

Separation and evidence

Interpretation is separated from unrestricted execution, and material actions preserve sufficient provenance for review.

Institutional architecture

Lawful possession is not lawful passage.

Enterprise AI Customs distinguishes legitimate control of information from legitimate authorization to move it. Internal data-domain transfers and external boundary crossings are evaluated through independently accountable Authority decisions, governing Law, and recorded evidence.

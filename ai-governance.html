One Framework. Two Options.

Choose the operating configuration.

Core Operation · Complete configuration

Human-governed consequential action

Establishes organizational governance, explicit Law, Authority boundaries, judicial oversight, independent protective enforcement, evidence, and assurance. People carry out every consequential business transaction.

Optional · XAA-Enabled Operation

Bounded AI-initiated transactions

Adds the Executive AI Assistant (XAA), an AI AGENT that may initiate specific, pre-enrolled transactions. Each one is checked against current authorization and judicial instruments at the moment it takes effect.

Institutional architecture

Lawful possession is not lawful passage.

Enterprise AI Customs distinguishes legitimate control of information from legitimate authorization to move it. Internal data-domain transfers and external boundary crossings are evaluated through independently accountable Authority decisions, governing Law, and recorded evidence.

Versioning

How v3.0.0 is updated.

This framework uses RELEASE.CORE.XAA: the first number marks a new framework generation, the second a change to common Core requirements, and the third a change limited to optional XAA requirements. Every edition keeps its own version and date, and earlier editions remain citable.

Terms used on this page

Core Operation
The complete configuration, in which people carry out every consequential action.
XAA-Enabled Operation
The optional configuration that adds the XAA for specific, pre-enrolled transactions.
EDA
Enterprise Domain Authority. The human sovereign root of a JAZB organization; every other Authority traces back to it.
AGENT
An AI system acting within a JAZB organization. The XAA and AA are AGENT roles.
XAA
Executive AI Assistant. An AI AGENT authorized to carry out specific consequential tasks, within the limits the EDA permits. Present only in XAA-Enabled Operation.
AA
AI Assistant. An AI AGENT that helps end users but does not execute consequential changes.
Law
The rules people establish through JAZB governance, defining what any actor, human or AI, may do.
Authority
Legitimate, human-established permission to act. Access, credentials and technical capability do not amount to Authority.
RELEASE.CORE.XAA
The AI framework’s version format: framework generation, then common Core revisions, then optional XAA revisions.

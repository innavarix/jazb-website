VERSION 1.0 | SEPTEMBER 2026

JAZB Framework v1.0

Version 1.0 was the initial public release of the complete seven-publication JAZB Framework. It established the human-sovereign Authority architecture, separation of Internal and Border Jurisdictions, Human-Established Law Layers, AI judicial and operational functions, Enterprise AI Customs, Governance Escape Resistance, implementation guidance, standards mapping, and the original normative Reference Specification.

JAZB Framework v1.0 has been superseded by v1.1 for current implementation and conformance work. It remains available as a historical release and may continue to be cited where reference to the original published architecture is required.

PUBLISHED LEGACY RELEASE | VERSION 1.4 | OCTOBER 2026

JAZB Framework Version 1.4

Version 1.4 is preserved as the last published release of the former v1.x numbering. It strengthened independent behavioral governance, active-Law verification, Customs, quarantine, resilience, and the AGENT autonomy boundary. Its 262 normative requirements remain applicable to v1.4-specific evaluation. The v1.x DOI lineage remains separate from the new v2.x series and may continue independently.

Phase I: Foundational Architecture

Establishes human sovereignty, organizational decision rights, Human-Established Law, and the four-level JAZB architecture. Version 1.4 corrects the AGENT autonomy boundary where consequential execution would depend on self-policing, and strengthens lawful Authority verification, accountability, and assurance.

THE GOVERNANCE GAP

Authority is the
missing governance layer.

Identity establishes who an actor is. Access determines what that actor can reach. JAZB addresses the next question: what may that actor legitimately do, for what purpose, under whose Authority, and under which active Law?

IDENTITYWho is the actor?

Authentication and identity establish who or what is acting.

ACCESSWhat can it reach?

Permissions and controls determine which resources are technically reachable.

AUTHORITYWhat may it legitimately do?

JAZB binds action to purpose, Scope, Law, delegation, provenance, and evidence.

CURRENT PUBLICATION FAMILY

Seven phases.
One canonical architecture.

JAZB Framework v2.0 is the current major-version publication family. It establishes parallel Internal Law domains under Human-Established Governor Law, separate Task and Enforcer AGENTs with independently verifiable supervisory Authority, bounded autonomous execution, independent consequential Actuation, expanded Enterprise AI Customs, and continuous assurance. The seven-phase architecture remains human-sovereign. Version 2.0 is published in an independent DOI series; v1.x releases remain citable and independently maintainable.

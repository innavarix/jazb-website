Origins

From JASB to JAZB

JASB began with purpose-bound elevated authority. JAZB expanded that inquiry into human-established Law, delegated authority, independent oversight, and verifiable enforcement.

Expansion

Enterprise cybersecurity governance

The Cybersecurity Framework is a separately versioned research family focusing on enterprise cybersecurity governance and assurance, not an XAA option or replacement for the original AI authority research.

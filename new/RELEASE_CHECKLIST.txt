JAZB portfolio staging release checklist
1. Review staging at https://jazbframework.org/new/ including desktop and mobile.
2. Do not publish unpublished Cybersecurity documents to the public /new folder.
3. Verify official publication DOI, contents, license metadata and repository record.
4. Replace ALL "pending" and "forthcoming" Cybersecurity edition wording only after publication, with exact DOI and release date.
5. Remove STAGING_ONLY_REMOVE_ON_PROMOTION robots noindex meta from all pages before promotion.
6. Preserve historical DOI links, legacy URL anchors and existing archive.html and crosswalk subpages.
7. Ensure root site links, sitemap.xml, canonical URLs and robots.txt reflect released site.
8. Tag or preserve the prior production commit. Promote site assets and files from new/ together, then verify root deployment and remove new/ staging.
9. Recheck publication-specific rights, editorial distinction between JAZB AI and cybersecurity families.
10. Never include internal original-name provenance or unvalidated claims in public documents.
